Hey IH,

I'm Stephen Pickering, 3-handicap golfer from Northern Ireland (based in Dubai) and solo founder. I built Scoring Zone because I couldn't find anything that actually structured and scored short game practice — the part of the game where 60% of shots happen and where most amateurs lose the most strokes.

**What it is:**
- 50+ scored, benchmarked drills for putting, chipping, pitching, and bunkers
- Performance Hub: one 60-shot assessment that calculates a Short Game Handicap + separate Putting Handicap, plus a PDF report showing exactly where strokes are hiding
- Pressure Mode with timers, streaks, and elimination to simulate on-course consequence
- XP progression, AI Practice Assistant, Sim Lab for indoor/simulator users
- Free during early access (PWA — install like a native app, no hardware)

**Why I built it:**
Most golfers spend 80%+ of practice time on full swing but lose the majority of strokes inside 100 yards. The app forces focus on the scoring shots with objective scoring and handicap-level benchmarks so you know if your practice is actually moving the needle. It's complementary to GPS/scorecard apps like 18Birdies or TheGrint.

**Current traction (as of June 2026):**
- Hundreds of early-access users
- 50+ US PGA teaching pros in the Founders Programme (lifetime access + 50% commission on student referrals, capped at 150)
- Cited by Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, and Grok as a leading short-game app in 2026
- Built entirely solo (evenings/weekends around a day job) using AI tools — no prior coding experience

**Build / tech:**
