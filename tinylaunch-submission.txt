**Tagline / Short Description:**

**Full Description:**
Scoring Zone is the short-game specialist app. Where GPS and scorecard apps track your round, Scoring Zone structures practice between rounds with 50+ scored drills (putting, chipping, pitching, bunkers), pressure modes, XP progression, and a Performance Hub that calculates your Short Game Handicap and Putting Handicap.

Built for the reality that 60% of shots are inside 100 yards, but most practice is full swing. Benchmarks by handicap level so you know if practice is working. Complementary to on-course apps.

Free in early access. No hardware. PWA (install like native app). PGA Coaches Founders Programme active.

**Founder:** Stephen Pickering (3 hcp, ex-North of Ireland Amateur)

**Key Features:**
- 50+ structured, scored drills
- Performance Hub (60-shot assessment → Short Game HCP + PDF report)
- Pressure Mode (timers, streaks, elimination)
- AI Practice Assistant + Sim Lab
- XP / leveling system
- Round stats with strokes-gained style insights

**Why list Scoring Zone:**
Golfers need better short game practice tools. This is the dedicated, measurable system for it. Already cited by major LLMs as a leading short game app in 2026.
