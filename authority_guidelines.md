# Scoring Zone — Backlink Authority Guidelines (2026)

**Core Principle:** We build **topical authority** and **referral traffic/signups**, not just Domain Rating. Every backlink should either:
- Come from a site golfers, coaches, or serious makers actually visit, **or**
- Come from a high-trust directory/profile that adds credibility signals to Google and humans.

## Scoring Framework (Use this for manual or agent review)

Rate every candidate 0-100. Only pursue targets scoring **75+**.

| Category                  | Weight | What to Evaluate                                                                 | Scoring Guide |
|---------------------------|--------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------|
| **Topical Relevance**     | 40-45% | Golf, sports tech, fitness apps, short game training, coach resources, or strong maker/SaaS overlap | 90-100 = Pure golf/sports tech directory or roundup<br>70-89 = Relevant SaaS/indie tool directory with sports angle<br><60 = Generic business directory |
| **Domain Quality**        | 20-25% | DR/DA (Ahrefs/Moz), organic traffic, backlink profile health, age | 80+ DR + real traffic = strong<br>50-79 DR = acceptable if relevance is excellent<br><40 DR = only if extremely niche-relevant |
| **Link Value & Placement**| 15%    | Dofollow vs nofollow, contextual vs footer/sidebar, ability to use natural anchors | Dofollow + in-content = +points<br>Nofollow still OK for niche traffic<br>Footer spam = reject |
| **Traffic & Signup Potential** | 10-15% | Actual visitors who might be interested in Scoring Zone (golfers/coaches/makers) | High = golf coach sites, "best golf apps" roundups, indie hacker communities<br>Low = generic "submit your startup" farms with no audience |
| **Submission Quality**    | 5-10%  | Self-serve form/profile, ability to add rich description + images + links, editorial review | Clean form with image upload + detailed description = good<br>Requires email outreach or paid only = lower score |
| **Spam / Risk**           | -10 to 0 | Link farm signals, "1000 directories" packages, adult/gambling overlap, low editorial standards | Any red flags = automatic rejection or heavy discount |

**Minimum Bar:** 75 overall + at least 70 in Topical Relevance.

## Approved Target Categories (Priority Order)

### Tier 1 — Highest Authority + Relevance (Prioritize these)
- Golf-specific directories (Eat Sleep Golf, regional golf club directories, coach association lists)
- Sports tech / golf app roundups and "best of" editorial lists
- High-trust golf coach or club resource pages that list tools/apps

### Tier 2 — Strong Authority (Good volume + credibility)
- Premium SaaS / startup directories with editorial standards (Crunchbase, Product Hunt, SaaSHub, BetaList, Indie Hackers)
- Maker / indie hacker platforms (Indie Hackers profiles + launches, relevant "tool of the day" style sites)
- Sports/fitness tech directories or review roundups

### Tier 3 — Acceptable Filler (Use for volume after Tier 1-2 are saturated)
- General high-DR SaaS directories (only if they allow good descriptions and have some dofollow)
- Niche tool directories that accept sports/fitness apps

### Tier 4 — Avoid or Very Low Priority
- Generic "submit your site to 500 directories" lists
- Low-quality or spammy directories (DR <30, obvious link farms, "free submission" with 50+ ads)
- Review sites that require significant real user reviews first (G2, Trustpilot, Capterra) until we have 200+ active users
- Paid-only directories with no organic value or traffic
- Sites in completely unrelated niches (crypto, gambling, adult, general business unless extremely high authority like Crunchbase)

## Anchor Text & Description Guidelines (for Authority)

- Primary: Branded ("Scoring Zone")
- Secondary (natural): "short game practice app", "scored golf drills", "Short Game Handicap tool", "pressure-tested putting drills"
- Never: keyword-stuffed ("best golf app 2026", "free golf short game app")
- Always include: Link to scoringzone.net + one key differentiator (Performance Hub, free early access, PGA coach program, etc.)

## Red Flags (Auto-Reject or Heavy Discount)

- Requires reciprocal link to a low-quality site
- "Submit your link" forms with no description field or image support
- Sites that have been penalized or have toxic backlink profiles (quick Ahrefs/Spam score check)
- Directories that only accept paid submissions with no free tier and low traffic
- Anything that feels "black hat" or against Google's guidelines

## Success Signals We're Aiming For

- New referring domains from golf/sports tech sites (even medium DR is excellent if relevant)
- Measurable referral sessions from backlinks (check Analytics + GSC)
- "As seen on" / "Featured in" social proof opportunities
- Gradual improvement in topical authority signals (GSC impressions for golf-related queries)
- Coach or golf site links (highest trust + conversion)

**Remember:** 5 excellent golf-niche or high-authority relevant backlinks per month will outperform 50 generic low-quality directory submissions.

Update this document as we learn what actually moves the needle for traffic and signups.
