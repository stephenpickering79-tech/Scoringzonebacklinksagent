## 2. Positioning (the canonical framing)

Every other golf app tells you what happened during your round (GPS, scorecards, shot tracking). Scoring Zone is the app that helps you fix it between rounds. It owns the **structured short-game practice** category — no hardware, no sensors, just scored drills with benchmarks against other golfers at your handicap level.

## 4. Product details

### Drills (50+ structured, scored)
Each drill has a points system, performance benchmarks, and progress tracking so golfers always know whether their practice is working.

**Putting drills (examples):**
- **Five-Foot Circle** — 10-in-a-row from 5 ft, 15-min timer; score = total putts taken
- **Lag King** — 10 putts from 40+ ft, measure proximity inside 3 ft
- **Clock Drill** — 8 putts in a circle 6 ft from the hole, no misses allowed
- **Speedmaster** — 5 putts from 30 ft, points based on finish proximity
- **Half Circle T-Drill** — 30–50 ft lag putting, must finish in T zone past the hole
- **Deathstar Drill** — 16 consecutive makes from 3/4/5/6 ft, 30-min cap
- **Century Drill** — 100 putts from mixed distances
- **Clutch Putt Challenge** — pressure putting from 3–6 ft

**Chipping & short-game drills (examples):**
- **The Ten-Yarder** — 10 chips from just off the green to a 10-yard hole; score by balls inside 3 ft
- **Ladder Up** — 4 chips through 5 gates 10–20 ft apart, miss a gate and restart
- **Par 2** — 9 chip-and-putt scenarios around the green, hole out in 2 or fewer
- **21 Points** — 5 points inside 1 ft, 3 inside 3 ft, 1 point per chip until 21 or bust
- **Flop Around** — chip over a 5-ft obstacle to a 15-ft target
- **15-Minute Blitz** — pressure chipping zones (5/10/15 ft) with one club
- **Up & Down Challenge** — 10 greenside positions, up-and-down percentage tracked
- **Gate Drill** — stroke accuracy through a narrow gate at varying distances

**Sim Lab (for golf simulator users):**
- **Dial-in 20** — distance control mastery
- **Pitching Accuracy Ladder** — pitching from various distances
- **Random Pitch Seeker** — 20 pitches from 50–100 yards
- **Chipping Proximity Gauntlet** — chips from 10–50 yards
- **Morris Short Game Challenge** — 30 shots, 10 each from 8/20/45 yards

### Modes & features
- **Performance Hub** — 60-shot assessment that calculates your **Short Game Handicap**; also includes Ultimate Putting Challenge, Clutch Putt Challenge, Century Drill, 15-Minute Blitz
- **Pressure Mode** — adds streaks, time limits, and elimination consequences to simulate on-course pressure
- **Short Game Handicap & Putting Handicap** — separate benchmark numbers that fall as you improve
- **Elite Mode** — tour-level analytics including strokes gained around the green and trend data; unlocked at higher XP levels
- **XP / progression** — drills unlock as you level up, with 1x/2x/3x multipliers; gamifies consistent practice
- **AI Practice Assistant** — guided 1-hour structured sessions (technical, performance, random practice), a clock-system wedge calculator (7 / 9 / 10 o'clock positions), practice notepad, pre-round warm-up routines
- **Round stats** — log every round at any course worldwide; after 2 rounds, get strokes-gained data benchmarked against your handicap. Approach heat maps, miss tendencies, FIR / GIR / up-and-down %, putts per round
- **Sim Lab** — indoor practice mode for golf simulators

### Pricing & access

## 5. Key stats / data we cite

- ~60% of golf shots happen inside 100 yards
- Most golfers spend 80%+ of practice on full swing (the inverse of where strokes are lost)
- 15-handicapper loses ~4–5 shots per round inside 50 yards vs. a scratch golfer
- PGA Tour average putts per round: 29. Average amateur: 36–40
- A 15+ handicapper three-putts 3.2 times per round on average; tour pros three-putt about once every 36 holes
- PGA Tour scrambling: 59%. Scratch amateur: 50%. 10-handicap: 32%. 20-handicap: 10%
- From 10 ft, PGA Tour pros make 40%; from 20 ft, ~15%
- Mark Broadie's strokes-gained data: the long game accounts for ~two-thirds of the scoring gap; putting only ~15%. **Accuracy into the green matters enormously — and the short game compounds every approach miss.**
