3. **Community & Forum Seeding (Authentic, High-Conversion)**

   - Founder story + "free tool that actually measures short game" resonates.
   - Focus on value-first posts (share a drill, benchmark insight, or the free Handicap Calculator), not pure promo.

4. **Editorial, Press & Guest Content (Authority + Reach)**
   - Leverage the polished journalist pitch email (v5).
   - Target UK golf media first (Bunkered, Today's Golfer, NCG, Golf Monthly) + US coach/college audiences.
   - Monthly Medium founder series (continue cadence started April 2026).

5. **Partnership & Coach Amplification (Warm, High-Trust Referrals)**
   - PGA Coaches Founders Programme (launched 6 Mar 2026, 50% commission, lifetime access, capped at 150).
   - Encourage coaches to link from their sites, include in student resources, feature in newsletters.
   - Coach testimonials + case studies become linkable assets.

6. **Linkable Assets & Tools (Earned Links Over Time)**
   - Free public tools: Golf Handicap Calculator (WHS/GHIN compliant).
   - Performance data / benchmarks ("what real 10-20 handicaps score on Lag King / Up & Down Challenge").
   - Comparison pages (already have 18 Birdies vs Scoring Zone).
   - Embeddable drill guides or short game improvement templates.
   - Founder story + "solo AI-built sports tech" angle for tech/golf crossover coverage.

## Automation with Steel.dev (CAPTCHA & Anti-Bot Bypass)

We now have reliable cloud browser automation via **Steel.dev** for anything that normally gets blocked by CAPTCHAs or bot detection.

**Why this matters for backlinks:**
- Most directory "get listed" forms, contact pages, and some review sites throw CAPTCHAs (ReCAPTCHA, Cloudflare Turnstile, etc.).
- Manual submissions are slow and inconsistent.
- Steel gives us **stealth fingerprinting + proxies + automatic CAPTCHA solving** in one package.
- Existing proven pattern already used successfully in the Competitions project (sweepstakes entry automation).

**Key files added:**
- `steel_utils.py` — Context manager `steel_page(solve_captcha=True)` + `wait_for_captchas()` + cheap `client.scrape()` helper. Directly adapted from `Documents/Grok/Competitions/steel_browser.py`.
- `.env.example` — Put your Steel key here as `STEEL_API_KEY` (the one you shared starts with `ste-ju8...`).

**How to use (Python):**
```python
from steel_utils import steel_page, wait_for_captchas

with steel_page(solve_captcha=True) as (playwright, browser, page, client, session_id):
    page.goto("https://www.eatsleepgolf.net/get-listed")
    # fill form fields using page.fill, page.click, etc.
    # Steel will auto-detect and solve most CAPTCHAs
    wait_for_captchas(client, session_id)
    # submit, take screenshot, extract confirmation, etc.
```

You also get a live **session viewer URL** printed every time — open it to watch the automation in real time (huge for debugging form flows).

**Other Steel superpowers available immediately:**
- `client.scrape(url=...)` — very cheap one-shot extraction (full HTML + clean markdown + readability text + links) without spinning up a full interactive browser session. Perfect for bulk discovery of "best golf apps 2026" pages.
- Persistent Profiles (cookies + auth across sessions).
- Files API, Chrome extensions, mobile emulation.
- Works with Playwright (current), Puppeteer, Selenium, and many AI agent frameworks (Browser Use, Claude/OpenAI Computer Use, LangGraph, CrewAI, etc.).
- Full docs: https://docs.steel.dev (CAPTCHA specifics: https://docs.steel.dev/overview/stealth/captcha-solving)
