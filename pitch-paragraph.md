# Scoring Zone — Directory / Review Site Pitch

Hi — I'm Stephen Pickering, founder of Scoring Zone. Big golf fanatic, been playing since my early teens. I built Scoring Zone after noticing a gap in the market — a lot of apps focus on GPS, scorecards, and full-swing analytics, but I couldn't find anything focused on the short game. Specifically, a performance-based app that gamifies and pressure tests your short game. In a nutshell, that's what Scoring Zone does.

The app is currently free and in early access for all users over the next few months. It's a PWA that can be saved to your phone and used like any native app. Every drill has a points system, performance benchmarks, and progress tracking so golfers always know whether their practice is working.

**Sample challenges include:**

- Ladder Putting — lag putts from increasing distances, scored on proximity
- Up & Down Challenge — chip and putt from 10 greenside positions, percentage tracked
- 3-2-1 Pressure Test — elimination-style putting drill that simulates closing out a round
- Gate Drill — stroke accuracy test through a narrow gate at varying distances
- 100-Putt Challenge — endurance test across mixed distances with a cumulative score

**Additional features:**

- **Practice Assistant** — a toolkit for better practice sessions, including a clock wedge distance calculator, practice notes, a session timer, and a pre-round warm-up routine
- **Elite Mode** — tour-level analytics including strokes gained around the green and trend data
- **Full Performance Tests** — full short game and putting assessments that calculate a separate Short Game Handicap and Putting Handicap

Features and challenges unlock progressively as you earn XP by completing drills — so the app rewards consistent practice with new content. To get the full experience of the progression system, it's best to work through the challenges from the start.

We also launched our PGA Coaches Founders Programme on 6th March, giving professional golf coaches early access to test the app, provide feedback, and shape the product alongside us as founding partners.

App Store and Google Play launches are planned once we've built a solid user base. I'd welcome the opportunity for Scoring Zone to be considered for your golf app coverage. Happy to provide screenshots, a demo walkthrough, or any additional information.
